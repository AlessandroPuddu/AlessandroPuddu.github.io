Primary engagement

Embedded senior engineer

For teams that need more than implementation capacity: an engineer who can understand the product, challenge assumptions, own technical decisions and still ship code.

I join an existing team and work inside its delivery rhythm. The scope can start with one difficult subsystem, but the value comes from being able to move across gameplay, tooling, platform constraints and supporting services when the real problem crosses boundaries.

  • Own a production area without creating a separate technical island.
  • Translate product and design goals into maintainable runtime systems.
  • Review architecture, debug difficult failures and improve observability.
  • Collaborate directly with designers, artists, QA and other engineers.
  • Document decisions and leave the codebase easier to operate.

Working model

Clarity before ceremony

No elaborate sales process. The first goal is to understand whether the problem, expectations and working style are a good match.

Step 01

Fit conversation

A 30-minute call to understand the product, current constraint, team and desired outcome. A written brief is optional.

Step 02

Context and access

If there is mutual fit, we agree the safest way to review the relevant context. NDA and repository access happen only when needed.

Step 03

Concrete starting scope

We define the initial responsibility, communication rhythm and evidence of progress. The scope can expand once the codebase proves what it needs.

Step 04

Delivery with visibility

Working code, explicit risks, documented decisions and regular demonstrations—so the team can see both progress and technical direction.

A strong fit

Teams that value ownership

  • You have a serious Unity product and need senior hands-on capacity.
  • The problem crosses gameplay, tools, platforms, networking or services.
  • You want an engineer who can enter an existing codebase without demanding a rewrite.
  • You value direct communication, technical transparency and maintainable delivery.
  • You are open to remote collaboration with an Italy-based engineer.
